            [ SETTINGS ]

            Keys stay in this browser. Policy lives in the contract.

            This treasury
            Two ways to read. "Bursar reading" is for the NEAR HACKATHON demo: the page sends the invoice through Bursar's relay, which holds a NEAR AI Cloud key paid by the team, forwards the bytes untouched and keeps nothing; the invoice is in transit on that relay before it enters the enclave, and daily caps apply. A company using Bursar brings its own NEAR AI Cloud key: the invoice then goes from this browser straight to the enclave and never passes through Bursar. The receipt and its checks are identical either way. The agent key can only call request_payment; the owner key signs vendors, orders, limits and approvals.

              Governance

              Without a DAO, the owner or an approver decides what is held. A Sputnik DAO gives the company a council: every held payment becomes a proposal the council votes on, in this console or in Trezu on mainnet. The treasury keeps a little NEAR for the proposal bonds.

              Sputnik v2 factory, 6 NEAR deposit on testnet. More than half of the council approves a proposal.

                Held payments open a proposal on this DAO. Council members vote in Attention; on mainnet the same proposals appear in Trezu. Adding council members later is itself a DAO proposal (not in this console yet).

                Bursar is software, not a payment service. Funds stay in the company's own contract, with the company's keys.
