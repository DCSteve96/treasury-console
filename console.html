    Step 3 of 3

    How your invoices are read

    Every invoice is read by a model inside a secure enclave (a TEE on NEAR AI Cloud) that signs what it read. Pick who pays for that reading.

    With Bursar reading, the PDF goes through Bursar's relay to the enclave; the relay holds the key and counts the readings. With your own key (from cloud.near.ai) the PDF goes from this browser straight to the enclave and never touches Bursar. The signed receipt and its checks are the same either way.

    Your keys stay in this browser. A wallet owner gets them back in any browser from the contract's vault, after signing with the wallet; a backup file is the second way, and the only one without a wallet.
    This browser holds no keys for this company, but its contract keeps a vault with them, locked by your wallet.

              Roles and DAO:

              Governance ?

              Without a DAO, the owner or an approver decides what is held. A Sputnik DAO gives the company a council: every held payment becomes a proposal the council votes on, in this console or in Trezu on mainnet. The treasury keeps a little NEAR for the proposal bonds.

              Sputnik v2 factory, 6 NEAR deposit on testnet, paid by the payer account. More than half of the council approves a proposal.

                Held payments open a proposal on this DAO. Council members vote in Attention; on mainnet the same proposals appear in Trezu. Adding council members later is itself a DAO proposal (not in this console yet).

                Bursar is software, not a payment service. Funds stay in the company's own contract, with the company's keys.
