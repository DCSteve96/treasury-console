Bursar pays a company's invoices without ever handing the AI a key. The reading happens in a NEAR AI Cloud enclave, a NEAR contract enforces the limits the owner set, and the only thing that moves USDC off NEAR is a restricted executor; on NEAR the contract pays the vendor itself.

01 · Three roles, one key each

Nobody in the loop can both decide and move money.

The AI reads

The invoice is rendered to images and read by a model in an Intel TDX enclave on NEAR AI Cloud. The answer comes back with an ECDSA signature over the exact bytes and an attestation bound to a fresh nonce. The agent that submits it holds one function-call key, for request_payment only.

🔒 It cannot sign a transfer🔒 It cannot change a cap or a vendor

The contract decides

The vendor must be registered by the owner. The payout on the invoice must equal the one on record, so the new-bank-details fraud dies here. Duplicates are refused, caps and budgets apply, orders are checked. At or above a threshold a person decides: the owner, an approver, or a Sputnik DAO council when the company has one.

🔒 It cannot read an invoice🔒 It cannot pay an unknown vendor

A dumb executor moves

NEAR vendors are paid by the contract in the same receipt. Vendors on Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Solana or Sui go through NEAR Intents: the executor brings a quote 1Click signed, and the contract verifies the signature, the recipient and the amount on chain before any USDC leaves. Every decision is an on-chain event.

🔒 It cannot choose who gets paid🔒 It cannot move more than the contract queued

03 · Verify it yourself

Change one byte. Watch it break.

Every reading carries a receipt: the exact request and response bytes, the enclave's signature, the attestation with the TDX quote and the nonce. This is the receipt behind payment #1 of the demo treasury, checked in your browser right now. Flip the switch to change one byte of the model's answer and run the checks again.

The same checks run from the terminal with bursar verify. The verifier does not yet replay the RTMR3 event log or check GPU evidence; the receipt keeps both. Verify your own receipt.

bursar verify receipt-01-acme-hosting-0923.json
