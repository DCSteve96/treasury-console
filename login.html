Continue with your NEAR wallet

Meteor, HOT, Intear, Nightly, Near Mobile and the other NEAR wallets. The wallet signs you in. If your account owns a treasury, the console opens it. If it owns none yet, Bursar creates one on the spot: Bursar pays for the accounts and the contract, you pay nothing.

No NEAR wallet yet? Meteor, Intear or Nightly take a minute to set up, and testnet accounts are free. On testnet, HOT and OKX are not available: they run on mainnet only.
